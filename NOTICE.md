Ported or adapted:
- `tools/install.py`: SDB installer (no Tizen Studio needed), from `tizen/install.py`.
- `tools/build.py`: from `tizen/build.py`.
- `src/js/services/apps-catalog.js`: the hidden-apps filter, title fixes and the
  shelf brand gradients, from `web/tv.js` and `web/home.js`.
- `src/js/services/launcher.js`: the DIAL fallback for native built-in apps, from `web/tv.js`.
- `src/js/core/platform.js`: the memory-based performance tiers (field data), from `web/tv.js`.
- `src/assets/icons/apps/*`: app logos, as distributed by Free My TV.
- `src/js/data/zones.js`: time zone → city table (generated by its `tools/make_zones.py`
  from the tz database, which is public domain).
- `src/js/services/inputs.js`: switching TV/HDMI inputs via `tvwindow.setSource` + the TV viewer.
- `src/js/services/weather.js`: Open-Meteo query and parsing approach.
- `src/js/services/picks.js`: the idea of TMDB picks limited to the services installed on the TV.
